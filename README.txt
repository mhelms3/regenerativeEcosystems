This is a self-contained static website. It uses no external libraries, fonts, trackers, or network requests.

The Ecosystem Operations section includes interactive drill-downs for Woody
Biomass Import & Separation, Kiln Drying & Products, Mushroom Block Production
& Grower Support, and Greenhouse Crop Production & Shared Processing. Each
presents material flows, economics, capital burden, data-center recovery value,
DeKalb County value, safeguards, and a municipal support test using consistent
accounting boundaries.

Version 5 adds an inter-service exchange ledger to all four operations. Each
ledger reports internal inputs and the OpEx they avoid, internal outputs and the
OpEx they may avoid elsewhere, and an explicit reconciliation note. Exchange
values are stand-alone planning statistics and are not added to service-line
revenue, operating surplus, or the consolidated ecosystem total. Provisional
ranges must be replaced with contracted transfer prices or verified
avoided-cost benchmarks.

Version 6 applies the July 31, 2026 funding-strategy update. It adds a main-page
four-line financial summary under the illustrative target capital stack,
updates annual debt service and post-debt cash results for every service line,
and reports direct data-center operating value separately from ecosystem
exchanges. The illustrative 35% host land/infrastructure capital contribution
is also kept separate from annual operating value and revenue.
